About me

I completed my PhD in the Nonlinear Algebra group at MPI MiS in Leipzig, under the supervision of Gleb Pogudin and Bernd Sturmfels. I defended my thesis, Combinatorial methods in differential algebra, in June 2023.

From 2023 to 2024, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the Institut de recherche en informatique fondamentale (IRIF) in Paris.

From 2024 to 2026, I was a postdoctoral research associate in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Oxford.
